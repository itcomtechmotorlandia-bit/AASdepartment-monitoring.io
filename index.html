Import Excel columns: Branch, Area, No. of Mechanics, Classification, Monthly Target, Week 1 Income to Week 5 Income (matching branches are updated, new ones are added). Ideal daily income = Monthly Target ÷ 26 working days. Type each week's income and the number of services repaired; Daily Service Income = week income ÷ 5.2; performance = week income ÷ monthly target (green ≥ 100%, amber ≥ 80%, red below). Totals: income = sum of the five weeks; daily service income = total income ÷ 26 (5 weeks × 5.2); performance = total income ÷ monthly target; Need to Achieve = monthly target − total income. Grades (1st, 2nd, 3rd) are set per branch in Manage Branches.
